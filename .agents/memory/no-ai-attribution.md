description: Keep commit authorship accurate and avoid unsolicited AI attribution.
metadata:
  type: project
---

Use the actual contributor's identity for new commits; do not impersonate an upstream author. Do not add unsolicited AI attribution or generated-with trailers.

Preserve existing upstream copyright and license notices. Do not rewrite published history as part of routine cleanup.

**Why:** Grabbit maintains its own changes while preserving accurate upstream attribution.
**How to apply:** review the author, committer, and message before publishing each commit.
